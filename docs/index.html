Flagship MSc dissertation

Explainable category-store demand forecasting and forecast governance

For my MSc dissertation, I used the M5 Forecasting - Accuracy dataset, a public Walmart item-sales dataset, and aggregated it into 30 category-store series across 1,941 days. I compared LightGBM, Holt-Winters and seasonal naive across four ordered 28-day backtests, producing 3,360 forecast points per model and 10,080 in total. LightGBM achieved 0.6528 RMSSE, 26.4% below seasonal naive and 10.6% below Holt-Winters. A five-page Power BI dashboard compares performance, shows the main model drivers and flags the series and horizons that need review.
