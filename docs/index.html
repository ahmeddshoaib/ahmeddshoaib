Flagship MSc dissertation

Explainable category-store demand forecasting and forecast governance

My MSc dissertation achieved 26.4% lower forecast error than a seasonal baseline across four time-ordered 28-day backtests on 30 retail demand series. I used public historical M5 data to compare LightGBM with Holt-Winters and seasonal naive. LightGBM achieved 0.6528 RMSSE, also 10.6% below Holt-Winters. A five-page Power BI dashboard compares performance and highlights series and horizons needing review. These are historical evaluation results, not realised inventory savings or a live employer deployment.

Client and team work

Craigavon TV: converting service records into an operational planning view

I initiated and led a completed collaborative engagement through Data Duo to connect repair jobs and appointments for service-workload reporting. The team produced a Power BI model and service-analytics reporting outputs, with documented data-quality limitations.

Data Duo · Craigavon TV

The reporting problem was to connect incoming repair demand with the appointments needed to serve it. Linking the two datasets helped distinguish job volumes from service touchpoints and structure discussion of workload, repeat activity and reporting definitions.

  • Initiated the engagement and led the project team through requirements, validation, analytical development and delivery.
  • Linked 28,115 repair jobs with 35,994 appointment records.
  • Fifty-nine of 60 documented data-quality checks passed across the two operational datasets; one unresolved check was retained as a reporting limitation.
  • The team delivered a Power BI model and analytical reporting outputs. Further definitions and data were identified for deeper service-performance and planning questions.

Completion refers to the project deliverables, not a claim of live production deployment, client adoption or measured efficiency savings. The technical build was shared; private client data and reports are not published here.
